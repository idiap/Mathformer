\section*{Simple harmonic oscillator angular frequency}
For an undamped oscillator satisfying $m\ddot{x}+kx=0$, use
$x=A\cos(\omega t)$ to derive
\[
  \omega=\sqrt{k/m}.
\]
Assume $m>0$ and $k>0$.
