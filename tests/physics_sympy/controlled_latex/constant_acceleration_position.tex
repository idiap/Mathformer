\section*{Constant acceleration position}
For one-dimensional motion with constant acceleration $a$, initial position
$x_0$, and initial velocity $v_0$, integrate velocity to obtain
\[
  x(t)=x_0+v_0t+\frac{1}{2}at^2.
\]
Assume $t\ge 0$ and use the positive $x$ direction.
