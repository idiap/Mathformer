\section*{Projectile range on level ground}
For projectile motion without drag and with launch speed $v_0$ at angle
$\theta$, level-ground range is
\[
  R=\frac{v_0^2\sin(2\theta)}{g}.
\]
Assume $g>0$ and upward positive vertical coordinate.
