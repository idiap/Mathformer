\section*{RC discharge}
For an ideal resistor-capacitor discharge,
\[
  \frac{dV}{dt}=-\frac{V}{RC},\qquad V(0)=V_0.
\]
The voltage is
\[
  V(t)=V_0 e^{-t/(RC)}.
\]
