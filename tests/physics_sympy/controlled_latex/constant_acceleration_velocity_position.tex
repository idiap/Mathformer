\section*{Velocity-position relation}
For constant acceleration in one dimension,
\[
  v=v_0+at,\qquad x=x_0+v_0t+\frac{1}{2}at^2.
\]
Eliminating time gives
\[
  v^2=v_0^2+2a(x-x_0).
\]
