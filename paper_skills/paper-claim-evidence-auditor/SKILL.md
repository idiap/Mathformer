# Paper Claim Evidence Auditor

## Purpose

Use this skill after drafting or major revision. It checks whether paper claims
are supported by local evidence and whether limitations are stated honestly.

## Workflow

1. Extract claims from the paper draft.
2. Classify each claim:
   - architectural;
   - mechanistic;
   - empirical/observational;
   - correctness/validation;
   - observability;
   - dashboard/interface;
   - workflow-control;
   - reasoning-log schema;
   - reference-free monitor;
   - workflow catalogue and graph reporting;
   - limitation;
   - future work.
3. Link each claim to evidence:
   - repository paths;
   - skill docs;
   - hook contract entries;
   - scripts;
   - generated reports;
   - Lean artifacts;
   - RFDE trajectory outputs.
   - workflow graph JSON/HTML, workflow layout plans, and workflow catalogue
     JSON/Markdown/LaTeX generated by the paper cartographer.
4. Flag overclaims:
   - correctness claims unsupported by Lean or consistency validation criteria;
   - empirical claims without artifacts;
   - generalization claims without multiple runs or benchmarks;
   - novelty claims without related-work support.
   - dashboard or RFDE claims stated as mathematical correctness claims;
   - figure/table claims without a generating path or explicit conceptual
     status;
   - claims that blur generated, validated, inspected, and conceptual artifacts.
   - workflow-count or skill-coverage claims not backed by the generated graph
     model or workflow catalogue.
5. Check that new claims include artifact paths when artifact evidence is
   required.
6. Rewrite unsupported claims as careful mechanism or proposal claims.

## Output Shape

Return:

- severity-ranked findings;
- claim-evidence table;
- suggested paper edits;
- residual risks.
